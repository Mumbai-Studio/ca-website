Privacy policy

How Collaborative Architecture collects and uses personal data through this website, its inquiry and newsletter forms, and the emails people send the studio. It applies wherever you live, and its Section 4 is our cookie policy.

1. About this policy

Who we are. This website is run by Collaborative Architecture, Mumbai Studio (“Collaborative Architecture”, “we”, “us”), G/4, Amit Industrial Estate, Dr. S S Rao Road, Lalbaug, Mumbai 400012, India. We decide how personal data collected through the website is used, as its controller (in India’s terms, its data fiduciary).

Privacy contact. Nitika, Coordinator, handles every question and request under this policy:

  • email: enquiry@collaborativearchitecture.com, with “Privacy” in the subject line;
  • telephone: +91 22 4004 7007;
  • post: the Mumbai Studio address above.

What it covers. Personal data we collect when you:

  • visit the website;
  • send an inquiry, including for a career or as a vendor;
  • subscribe to our newsletter;
  • write to us at one of our published email addresses.

It does not cover our project work for clients, which follows the terms of each engagement. Nor does it cover other websites we link to, such as Instagram, LinkedIn and YouTube, which have their own policies.

Which law applies. We follow this policy for everyone, wherever they live. Where the law of your country gives you more, Section 10 sets out what it adds, and that law prevails.

6. International transfers

We are based in India, and our team in Riyadh answers inquiries for the Riyadh studio. Our providers keep data in the United States and elsewhere (Section 5). So your personal data may be stored and used outside your country, including in countries whose laws protect it less than yours.

When we transfer personal data across borders, we do it only as your country’s law allows. Each transfer is protected, for example:

  • From the EU, EEA, UK or Switzerland: an adequacy decision, or the standard contractual clauses approved for such transfers.
  • From Saudi Arabia: a destination SDAIA recognizes as adequate, or the appropriate safeguards of the Transfer Regulation under the PDPL, such as standard contractual clauses.
  • From other countries with transfer rules: the mechanism that law sets. That may be a contract, a transfer impact assessment, a regulator’s authorization, or your separate consent where the law requires it (Section 10).

For a copy of the safeguards that apply to your data, write to Nitika at enquiry@collaborativearchitecture.com.

8. How we protect it

Our principles. We handle personal data:

  • lawfully, fairly and openly;
  • for the purposes stated, and no more than they need;
  • accurately, and only for as long as needed (Section 7);
  • securely.

We are accountable for meeting these principles, and we consider privacy from the start whenever we change the website or our systems.

Security measures.

  • The website is served only over encrypted connections (HTTPS), with modern security settings.
  • Every message sent through a form is checked again on our server.
  • The copies of messages we keep are in a folder closed to the internet.
  • Passwords and keys are kept out of the website’s public code.
  • Our email accounts use two-step sign-in.
  • Access to personal data is limited to the people who need it for their work, and they are bound to keep it confidential.
  • Our service providers are bound by contract to protect it.

No transmission over the internet can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Please do not send us information you consider highly confidential through the website.

If something goes wrong. If a breach of security puts personal data at risk, we act at once to contain it. We notify the relevant authority and the people affected, as and when the law requires.
