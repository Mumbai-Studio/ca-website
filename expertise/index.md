# Expertise

We work at every scale, from urban systems to the finest architectural detail. We serve private, corporate and public clients through our integrated approach to the built environment.

## [Architecture](https://collaborativearchitecture.com/expertise/architecture/)

Designed for enduring relevance. Collaborative Architecture designs buildings and campuses for education, work, research, culture, worship, and civic life.

## [Interior Architecture](https://collaborativearchitecture.com/expertise/interior-architecture/)

Architecture at the human scale. Interior architecture shapes how architecture is experienced at close range. We conceive interiors as an extension of the building, where materiality, light, acoustics, proportion, and spatial relationships inform the experience of place.

## [Urban Design and Infrastructure](https://collaborativearchitecture.com/expertise/urban-design-and-infrastructure/)

Infrastructure as a framework for urban life. Infrastructure shapes how cities function, connect, and evolve. From bridges and transit networks to riverfronts and public spaces, we explore how large-scale interventions can extend beyond utility to become civic landmarks and sources of collective identity.

## [Narrative Environments and Installations](https://collaborativearchitecture.com/expertise/narrative-environments-and-installations/)

Space as a medium for storytelling. We design environments that communicate through spatial experience. Retail spaces, galleries, museums, and experience centers become narratives shaped by sequence, light, material, sound, and movement.

## [Products and Objects](https://collaborativearchitecture.com/expertise/products-and-objects/)

Objects emerging from architectural inquiry. Many of our products originate in the specific demands of architectural projects, translating spatial concepts into objects with an independent design identity.

## [Design and Behavioral Mapping](https://collaborativearchitecture.com/expertise/design-and-behavioral-mapping/)

Human behavior as a generator of space. We investigate how people live, work, interact, move, and occupy space before design begins.
