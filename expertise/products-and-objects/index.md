# Products and Objects

Objects emerging from architectural inquiry. An expertise of Collaborative Architecture.

Many of our products originate in the specific demands of architectural projects, translating spatial concepts into objects with an independent design identity. Huddle seating and the 53 light, developed for Reflected Topography, and the Thousand Moons fixtures at Mezban exemplify this process. Supported by Collaborative Lab’s material research and prototyping, these explorations extend into furniture, lighting, objects, and jewelry.

## From cultural reference to contemporary production

Drawing from the murals and eroded landscapes of AlUla, Saudi Arabia, the Cliff Chair and Ripple jewelry collection translate cultural and geological references into contemporary forms. The Clip Chair explores the interplay of polycarbonate and bent plywood across different finishes and configurations. Through ongoing collaborations with Italian manufacturers, we continue to develop furniture that connects design research, material experimentation, and the possibilities of production.
