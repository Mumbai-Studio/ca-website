# Urban Design and Infrastructure

Infrastructure as a framework for urban life. An expertise of Collaborative Architecture.

Infrastructure shapes how cities function, connect, and evolve. From bridges and transit networks to riverfronts and public spaces, we explore how large-scale interventions can extend beyond utility to become civic landmarks and sources of collective identity. Our proposals include work developed with Henning Larsen for the Musi River in Hyderabad and with J. MAYER H. for a national landmark in Manila.

## Livability, ecology, and cultural identity

Our urban work examines the interplay of livability, ecology, mobility, and cultural context. We seek opportunities to restore natural systems, strengthen urban connections, and create public realms that respond to the character of their surroundings. By integrating infrastructure with the social and ecological fabric of a place, we aim to shape cities that are more connected, resilient, and meaningful to the communities they serve.
