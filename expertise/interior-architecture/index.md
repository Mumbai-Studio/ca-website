# Interior Architecture

Architecture at the human scale. An expertise of Collaborative Architecture.

Interior architecture shapes how architecture is experienced at close range. We conceive interiors as an extension of the building, where materiality, light, acoustics, proportion, and spatial relationships inform the experience of place. Through behavioral mapping, spatial analysis, and collaborative workshops, we translate our clients’ aspirations and patterns of use into tailored environments. Our work spans workplaces, restaurants, hotels, residences, showrooms, and experience centers, integrating every layer of the interior—from lighting and furniture to the sequences through which spaces are encountered.

## Designed in collaboration

Our clients are active partners in the design process, bringing an understanding of their lives, ambitions, and operations that informs our design expertise. We synthesize these insights with research into human behavior, materials, environmental performance, and cost to create spaces that respond holistically to their users and purpose. Designed for a long life cycle, our interiors prioritize durable materials, efficient systems, adaptability, and reduced operating costs.
