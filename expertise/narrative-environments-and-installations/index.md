# Narrative Environments and Installations

Space as a medium for storytelling. An expertise of Collaborative Architecture.

We design environments that communicate through spatial experience. Retail spaces, galleries, museums, and experience centers become narratives shaped by sequence, light, material, sound, and movement. Working with brands and cultural institutions, we translate products, ideas, and histories into immersive experiences that unfold as visitors move through space. Our collaborations include MillerKnoll, Titan, Hyundai, Stanley, and Moroso.

## Temporary architecture as a laboratory

Pavilions, prototypes, and installations provide a platform to test architectural ideas at full scale, exploring their spatial, material, and experiential potential before they inform permanent environments. Prototype N[ON] for Moroso and Wedge-1 for Stanley exemplify this experimental approach. Through the Black-Box Series, Collaborative Lab extends this inquiry into spatial speculations that challenge conventions and explore new architectural possibilities.
