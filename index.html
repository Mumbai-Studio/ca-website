Design beyondthe brief

Since 2004, Collaborative Architecture has been advancing a research-led approach to the built environment, including architecture and interiors, creating distinctive work shaped by culture, context and material intelligence.

Our projects evolve out of a meaningful convergence of people, places, culture, climate, ecology and economics.

Clients are our collaborators. Along with international teams of engineers, consultants and building scientists, our clients and end-users actively engage and collaborate to create architecture that performs and stays relevant for decades.
