# Architecture in Bengaluru

> Collaborative Architecture in Bengaluru (Bangalore): the Reflected Topography lobby, the MillerKnoll Experience Center, an NTT Data center and Rasa Totem.

In Bengaluru the studio designed Reflected Topography, the public lobby of the Bearys Global Research Triangle, lit by 53 custom lights set into its waffle slab; the MillerKnoll Experience Center, a net-zero experience center; a data center for NTT Data; and Rasa Totem, a 20-meter sculpture with the artist Riyas Komu, proposed for the new terminal of Kempegowda International Airport.

## Projects

- [Reflected Topography](https://collaborativearchitecture.com/work/reflected-topography/): Bengaluru, India, 2014, Workplace
- [MillerKnoll Experience Center](https://collaborativearchitecture.com/work/miller-knoll-experience-center/): Bengaluru, India, 2025, Experience center
- [Rasa Totem](https://collaborativearchitecture.com/work/rasa-totem/): Bengaluru, India, 2022, Art installation
- NTT Data Center: Bengaluru, India, 2019, Digital infrastructure
