# Architecture in Delhi NCR

> Collaborative Architecture in New Delhi and Delhi NCR: the Stanley Lifestyle Store, Peace Pavilion for the National War Museum and Concept Pavilion II.

In Delhi NCR the studio designed the Stanley Lifestyle Store; Peace Pavilion, its finalist proposal in the competition for the Indian National War Museum, later exhibited in New York and San Francisco; and Concept Pavilion II for Titan. The Stanley store presents the brand’s home and workplace collections across about 2,300 square meters. Lalita Tharani’s installation Levitation was shown at the DX Show in New Delhi in 2019.

## Projects

- [Peace Pavilion](https://collaborativearchitecture.com/work/peace-pavilion/): New Delhi, India, 2017, Museum, Civic
- Concept Pavilion II: New Delhi, India, 2006, Retail
- [Stanley Lifestyle Store](https://collaborativearchitecture.com/work/stanley-lifestyle-store/): Delhi NCR, India, 2009, Retail
