# Architecture in Kerala

> Eleven projects in Kochi, Kozhikode and across Kerala: show apartments for Skyline Builders and PVS Developers, schools, homes, restaurants and showrooms.

Kerala is where the studio has built most: eleven projects in Kochi, Kozhikode and across the state. They include Stacked Tectonics, a primary school built by village workers; Inverted Topography, the Mezban restaurant; House of Cantilevers, a family villa; Wrap-4 for KTC Hyundai; and The Legend, a show condominium in Kochi. The principals also contributed to the Design Policy for the State of Kerala, the first such policy adopted by an Indian state.
