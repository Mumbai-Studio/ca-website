# Architecture in Mumbai

> Collaborative Architecture in Mumbai and Navi Mumbai: a sea-facing apartment at Cuffe Parade, a research campus, a data center and an industrial conversion.

Collaborative Architecture is headquartered in Mumbai and has worked across the city and in Navi Mumbai: Apartment by the Bay, a 180-square-meter home overlooking the sea at Cuffe Parade; Wedge-1, an installation for Stanley; Prototype N[ON] for Moroso; the BGRT Mumbai research campus; a data center for NTT Data; and The Barn, an industrial shed converted into a workspace for Suretech Infrastructure.

## Projects

- [Wedge-1](https://collaborativearchitecture.com/work/wedge-1/): Mumbai, India, 2009, Retail
- [BGRT Mumbai](https://collaborativearchitecture.com/work/bgrt-mumbai/): Navi Mumbai, India, In design, Research campus
- NTT Data Center: Navi Mumbai, India, Three phases completed, Digital infrastructure
- [The Barn](https://collaborativearchitecture.com/work/the-barn/): Navi Mumbai, India, 2026, Adaptive reuse
