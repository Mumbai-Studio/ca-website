Architecture in Saudi Arabia

Collaborative Architecture SA is the studio’s office in Riyadh. It brings the Mumbai studio’s experience of workplaces, cultural buildings, hospitality and narrative environments to clients across the Kingdom, from Riyadh and Jeddah to the Eastern Province and the giga-project regions. The Cliff Chair and the Ripple jewelry collection take their forms from the murals and eroded landscape of AlUla. From Riyadh, the studio also serves clients across the GCC, Egypt and Morocco.
