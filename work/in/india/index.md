# Architecture in India

> Collaborative Architecture works across India from its Mumbai headquarters, with projects in Mumbai, Bengaluru, Kerala, Delhi NCR, Hyderabad and Chennai.

Collaborative Architecture was founded in Mumbai, where it is headquartered, and has designed projects across India: schools and homes in Kerala, workplaces and experience centers in Bengaluru, research campuses and data centers in Mumbai and Navi Mumbai, retail and pavilions in Delhi NCR, a river corridor with Henning Larsen in Hyderabad and a corporate office in Chennai. The studio takes on commissions in every metropolitan and tier 2 city.
