# Apartment by the Bay

Private residence, Mumbai. Mumbai, India, 2014. Design: Collaborative Architecture.

Located in Cuffe Parade, Mumbai, Apartment by the Bay is a 180-square-meter family residence completed in 2014. Set within one of the city’s sought-after residential buildings, the apartment opens to panoramic views of the Arabian Sea. The Interior Architecture balances a restrained minimalist language with a highly personalized approach, translating the clients’ refined aesthetic sensibilities and outgoing lifestyles into a home shaped around their individual needs.

## Key facts

- Location: Cuffe Parade, Mumbai, India
- Client: Private
- Typology: Residential, Interior
- Status: Completed
- Year: 2014
- Area: 180 m²
- Lighting design: Collaborative Architecture

## Minimal, and personal

Designed for a young family, the residence explores how minimalism can accommodate individuality without sacrificing spatial clarity. Rather than imposing a predetermined design language, the interiors are customized around the family’s distinct habits, preferences, and patterns of living. The result is an understated yet expressive domestic environment in which considered simplicity provides the framework for personal identity and everyday life.
