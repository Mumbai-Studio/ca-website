# Wedge-1

Stanley, Mumbai. Mumbai, India, 2009. Design: Collaborative Architecture.

Wedge-1 is a full-scale prototype for a freestanding luxury retail building for Stanley Lifestyles. Built for an exhibition in Mumbai, it tested how spatial form, product display and visitor movement could shape a more experiential retail environment. The experiment became a precursor to the Stanley Lifestyle Showroom.

## Key facts

- Location: Mumbai, India
- Client: Stanley Lifestyles
- Typology: Retail, Prototype
- Status: Completed
- Year: 2009
- Area: 300 m²
- Lighting design: Collaborative Architecture
- 5 awards and international shortlists

## A box stretched, cut and folded

The design transforms a simple rectangular volume into a pinwheel of trapezoidal spaces arranged around a central gathering area. Openings in the perimeter wall alternately reveal and conceal the displays, drawing visitors inward. Above, 1,200-millimeter T5 lamps set at shifting angles form a canopy of light that completes the interior.

## From prototype to recognition

Wedge-1 allowed the studio to explore how retail could be conceived as an architectural experience, rather than a sequence of displays. The project won Project of the Year at Architecture Israel in 2012 and was a finalist in the 2011 FX International Interior Design Awards. It was also published internationally, including by [ArchDaily](https://www.archdaily.com/94905/wedge-1-collaborative-architecture).

The project demonstrates how a focused design experiment can inform a larger retail concept, connecting spatial organization, display and visitor experience.
