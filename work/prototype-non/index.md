# Prototype N[ON]

Retail concept for Moroso, Mumbai. Mumbai, India, 2008. Design: Collaborative Architecture.

Temporary architecture serves as a testing ground for ideas that inform the evolving body of work at Collaborative Architecture. Conceived as spatial experiments, these prototypes provide a framework for testing, refining and reconfiguring architectural propositions before their translation into larger-scale applications. Prototype N[ON] is an early manifestation of this research-driven approach, establishing a lineage of experimental constructs that continues to inform the studio’s practice.

Presented as part of a design showcase organized by Universal Expo and Index Media at their 2008 trade exhibition in Mumbai, India, the project brought together ten prominent architectural practices, each invited to develop a conceptual and spatial intervention within a 5 × 5-meter footprint.

Conceived as a prototypical retail interface for the Italian furniture brand Moroso, Prototype N[ON] explored the possibilities of retail space as an evolving spatial construct. Rather than approaching the brief as a conventional display environment, the project used the pavilion as a testing ground for alternative spatial strategies, examining how a limited footprint could accommodate new relationships between enclosure, display and spatial experience.

## Material Logic and Construction

The project’s material and construction logic reinforces its experimental premise. The tectonic elements are weaved to create the space. Simple MDF panels, supported by mild-steel frameworks, were prefabricated and assembled on site, with an acrylic emulsion finish unifying the assembly. The resulting system demonstrates how a deliberately restrained palette of materials and straightforward construction techniques can support an exploratory architectural proposition.
