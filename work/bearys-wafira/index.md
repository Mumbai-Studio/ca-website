# Bearys Wafira

Residences, Dubai Healthcare City. Dubai, UAE. Design: Collaborative Architecture.

In Dubai Healthcare City, Bearys Wafira is conceived as a limited-edition residence where sophisticated luxury is shaped by its arid setting. Collaborative Architecture’s interior design spans the grand lobby, club floors, four condominiums and penthouse, bringing together a human-centered approach, considered detailing and a refined palette of bespoke finishes.

Bearys Wafira is due for completion in 2029. More details will follow.

## Key facts

- Location: Dubai Healthcare City, Dubai, United Arab Emirates
- Client: Bearys Global
- Typology: Residential, Interior
- Scope: Grand lobby, club floors, four condominiums and penthouse
- Status: Under construction, due for completion in 2029
- Architect: NMS, Calicut
- Resident architect: Arif & Bintoak, Dubai
- Consultants: LET, Dubai (lighting design); SEED, Dubai (building services); Fencon (façade); Excom, Dubai (cost); ARSA, India (structure); Acoustic Logic LLC, Dubai (acoustics)
- 4 condominiums and a penthouse
- Universal access, part of the design approach

## Bespoke interiors

Custom-developed finishes and a bespoke kitchen give the residences a distinct material character. Carefully resolved details carry this language across the interiors, balancing precision and refinement with the everyday experience of living.

## Spaces for living together

Public and communal areas are designed to encourage encounter and support a connected residential community. From the lobby to the club floors, shared spaces give social life a central place in the project.

## Integrated systems

The project combines design and material innovation with advanced systems for lighting, automation, water and solid-waste management, alongside biometric, IT and communication infrastructure. Universal access is also part of the design approach.

Wafira brings sophisticated residential design into dialogue with environmental performance, bespoke craftsmanship and the experience of space.
