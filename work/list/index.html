The Iconic Gateway

Muharraq, Bahrain · 2023 · Competition proposal

The Iconic Gateway marks the entrance to Diyar Al Muharraq in Bahrain. Conceived as both a pedestrian bridge and a landmark within the emerging urban landscape, it establishes a distinctive threshold to the new city. Drawing inspiration from Bahrain’s maritime heritage and the interlocking arches of the Diyar identity, the design synthesizes cultural symbolism with structural innovation, engineered to respond to the climatic demands of the Gulf.

Prototype N[ON]

Mumbai, India · 2008 · Completed

Temporary architecture serves as a testing ground for ideas that inform the evolving body of work at Collaborative Architecture. Conceived as spatial experiments, these prototypes provide a framework for testing, refining and reconfiguring architectural propositions before their translation into larger-scale applications. Prototype N[ON] is an early manifestation of this research-driven approach, establishing a lineage of experimental constructs that continues to inform the studio’s practice.

The Legend

Kochi, India · 2018 · Completed

The Legend is a 3,800-square-foot show condominium developed for Skyline Builders in Kochi to demonstrate the potential of design-led luxury residential development in South India. Conceived around the principle that good design can strengthen commercial value, the project uses Interior Architecture as a tool to improve not only the experience of the home, but also the efficiency, desirability, and market positioning of the condominium. The layout, material language, furniture strategy, and kitchen were developed as an integrated proposition for a broad prospective market.

The Barn

Navi Mumbai, India · 2026 · Completed

The Barn transforms an industrial shed in Navi Mumbai into a contemporary workspace for Suretech Infrastructure, retaining elements of its original workshop, paint shop, and mechanical repair functions. Conceived alongside the client’s ambitions to rebrand and expand its business, the project demonstrates how a modest budget can become a catalyst for architectural reinvention. Rather than erasing the building’s industrial origins, the intervention builds upon its existing character, recalibrating its material expression and spatial organization to establish a renewed corporate identity.

BGRT Mumbai

Navi Mumbai, India · In design

Commissioned by the Bearys Group, BGRT Mumbai is a 40,000-square-meter campus for general and pharmaceutical research. Building on the group’s Bengaluru campus, home to Reflected Topography, the proposal retains the masterplan’s footprint while reworking its architectural expression through a climate-responsive façade strategy. The project targets LEED Platinum certification.

Apartment by the Bay

Mumbai, India · 2014 · Completed

Located in Cuffe Parade, Mumbai, Apartment by the Bay is a 180-square-meter family residence completed in 2014. Set within one of the city’s sought-after residential buildings, the apartment opens to panoramic views of the Arabian Sea. The Interior Architecture balances a restrained minimalist language with a highly personalized approach, translating the clients’ refined aesthetic sensibilities and outgoing lifestyles into a home shaped around their individual needs.

Cascadia

Metro Manila, Philippines · 2017 · Finalist, two-stage international competition

Cascadia was a finalist entry in the two-stage international competition for the BCDA Iconic Tower, organized by the Government of the Philippines and the Bonifacio Development Authority in Manila. Designed by Collaborative Architecture in collaboration with J. MAYER H. und Partner Architekten, Berlin, the 40,000-square-meter proposal envisioned a national landmark for Bonifacio’s emerging central business district, transforming a former military site into a contemporary urban destination. The project explores how a high-rise development can extend beyond commercial functions to support cultural identity, public life, resilience, and sustainability.

Reflected Topography

Bengaluru, India · 2014 · Completed

Reflected Topography is the public lobby of the Bearys Global Research Triangle, a research and development campus in Bengaluru housing facilities for international companies. Within a 32 × 32-meter hall, the project integrates reception, waiting, relaxation, circulation, and private meeting spaces into a unified architectural experience. Conceived as an extension of the campus’s research-driven ethos, the design explores how structure, light, and spatial organization can transform a multifunctional interior into a coherent and dynamic public realm.

National Unity Pavilion

India · 2021 · One of the three winning entries, international competition

The National Unity Pavilion is Collaborative Architecture’s winning proposal for Notions of India, an international competition organized by Tata Steel to commemorate 75 years of Indian independence. Selected as one of three winning entries from 575 submissions, the project asks a fundamental question: can architecture give spatial form to the idea of a nation shaped by multiple histories, cultures, beliefs, and languages?

The Souk

Dubai, UAE · 2019 · Competition winner (unbuilt)

Selected as the winner of Dubai Design Week’s 2019 installation program, The Souk reimagines the traditional Middle Eastern bazaar as a contemporary public realm. Three identical timber pavilions form an interconnected cluster around a shared square, creating an urban microcosm where commerce, social exchange, and collective activity shape the spatial experience.
