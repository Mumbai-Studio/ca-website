Awards

60

awards, honors and international shortlists since 2004, 30 of them international

The studio’s work has been exhibited at the Cooper Hewitt, Smithsonian Design Museum in New York and the Museum of Craft and Design in San Francisco, nominated for the Aga Khan Award for Architecture, and published in journals and books across Asia, Europe and the Americas. Our work was included in the State of Architecture exhibition, at the National Gallery of Modern Art, Mumbai, among the 40 significant works in post-independent India.

Exhibition · 2022–2023

Designing Peace

The Peace Pavilion was one of 40 projects selected by the Cooper Hewitt, Smithsonian Design Museum for its exhibition in New York, which traveled to the Museum of Craft and Design in San Francisco.
